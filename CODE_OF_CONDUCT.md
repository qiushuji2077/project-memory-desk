# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Project Memory Desk a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying clear about problems.
- Keep feedback focused on the project.
- Protect privacy when discussing local-first workflows.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private project data, customer materials, or identifying records without permission.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, or confidential files in issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates this code. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a ban from project spaces.
